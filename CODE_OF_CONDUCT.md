# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include using welcoming and inclusive language.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible and accountable for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported.
